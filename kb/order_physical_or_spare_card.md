# Ordering a physical, spare or virtual card
You can order your first physical card for free in the app under Cards > Order card. Each customer can hold up to 2 physical cards; a spare card costs 100 EGP. Virtual cards can be created instantly in the app for free and used for online payments. Disposable virtual cards generate new card details after every payment for extra security.
