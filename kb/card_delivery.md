# Card delivery
New and replacement physical cards are delivered within 3 to 5 working days inside Cairo and Giza, and 5 to 7 working days in other governorates. Delivery is free. You can track your card in the app under Cards > Track delivery. If your card has not arrived after 10 working days, contact support so we can cancel it and send a new one.
