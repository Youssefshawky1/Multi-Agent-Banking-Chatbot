# Changing or unblocking your PIN
You can change your PIN at any NileBank ATM: insert your card, choose PIN Services, then Change PIN. You can also view your PIN securely in the app under Cards > Show PIN after verifying with your passcode or fingerprint. If you enter the wrong PIN 3 times, the PIN is blocked. To unblock it, go to Cards > Unblock PIN in the app and then use the card at any NileBank ATM.
