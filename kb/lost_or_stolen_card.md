# Lost or stolen card
If your card is lost or stolen, freeze it immediately in the app (Cards > Freeze). If you are sure it is gone, choose Cards > Report lost or stolen; this cancels the card permanently and orders a free replacement. Any card payments you do not recognise should be reported to the billing team. You can also call the 24/7 hotline 19999 to block your card.
