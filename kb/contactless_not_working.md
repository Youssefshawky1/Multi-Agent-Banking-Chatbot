# Contactless not working
Contactless payments are limited to 600 EGP per transaction. For security, after 5 contactless payments in a row you must insert the card and enter your PIN once. A brand-new card must be used once with chip and PIN before contactless is activated. Contactless can be switched on or off in Cards > Security settings in the app.
