# Verifying your identity
To open an account you must verify your identity with your national ID card or passport and a selfie in the app. Verification usually takes a few minutes but can take up to 24 hours. We verify identity because banking regulations require it to prevent fraud and money laundering. If verification fails, make sure the photo is clear, not blurred, and that the whole document is visible, then try again.
