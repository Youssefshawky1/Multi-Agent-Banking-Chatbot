# Forgotten app passcode or login problems
If you forgot your app passcode, tap Forgot passcode on the login screen. You will receive a one-time code by SMS to your registered phone number, then you can set a new 6-digit passcode. If you changed your phone number, you must verify your identity again before logging in. If the app crashes or will not open, update it to the latest version from the App Store or Google Play and restart your phone.
